PerUse: Cost per Use Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 26, 2026

Information used by the app

Cost per Use stores the item names, prices, types, billing cycles, start dates, target costs, usage dates and notes, archive state, and photos you attach in the app's local storage. A setting that remembers the decision about adding initial samples is stored in the app's own preferences. No account is required.

The selected sample or personal-record view and item identifiers used to detect new registrations are also stored in the app's own preferences. Existing sample identifiers keep samples separate from your recording queue and totals. Switching views does not delete items or history. You can choose to show samples again or restore missing samples.

This information is used to calculate cost estimates, display items and insights, show usage history, and display attached photos. Archiving does not change an external service contract. The app does not obtain the actual status of your subscription contracts.

Photos and camera

The photo picker reads an image you choose. The camera is used when you choose to take a photo. Attached images are normalized for orientation and size, converted to JPEG, and stored with an item when you save. The app does not modify original photos in your library or automatically save camera images to your photo library.

Name and amount recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on your device. Suggestions can contain errors. There is no feature that sends images to a developer server for text recognition. You can also enter items manually without attaching photos. You can change camera permission in iOS Settings.

Transfers and third-party services

The current app has no feature that sends item records, usage history, or attached images to a developer server. It includes no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK and no tracking feature. Third-party account sign-in and an app-managed cloud synchronization service are not implemented.

The initial release is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. A future Pro preview retained in development Debug builds has no payment or data-transmission implementation. Release builds have no entry to that preview.

Photo retrieval, diagnostics, and device backups performed by Apple's OS features follow your device settings and Apple's policies. App data may be included in device backups. Local app storage does not mean that OS backups have been disabled.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple's distribution, diagnostics, and feedback services are separate from the app's own handling of item records. When you choose to submit feedback through TestFlight, the comments, screenshots, and other materials you send are shared with Apple and the developer. Beta diagnostics and usage information are handled as described in Apple's TestFlight information. Do not include personal photos, real receipts, or credentials in feedback. You may also use the support contact on this page for email inquiries.

Retention and deletion

Saved records remain in app storage until you delete them in the app or remove the app data through the OS. Deleting an item removes the item and related usage history from the app. Reset all data removes the app's items and usage history together. These actions cannot be undone; review the confirmation before proceeding.

Reset all data includes both personal records and samples. Samples are not automatically reinserted after a reset. You can add them again explicitly through Show samples in Manage data. Switching the displayed records or archiving an item is different from deleting it.

Deleting app records does not delete existing OS backups or original images you saved separately. Manage backups through your OS settings. The developer has no feature for remotely obtaining or restoring your local records.

Support requests

If you contact support, the contact details, message, and materials you choose to send will be used to respond to and investigate the issue. Redact personal, financial, and unrelated information from receipts and photos.

Support email uses Google Gmail. The email address, message and attachments you choose to send are retained for as long as needed to reply and investigate the issue. When handling the request ends, the need to retain the information is reviewed and information no longer needed is deleted. To request deletion, email the contact on this page with enough information to identify the correspondence. Google processes messages for delivery and storage; its retention and handling after deletion follow Google’s policies. You can use the app without sending an email.

In-app information and public web pages

Privacy and data in the app includes offline technical information about local storage and related features. This page provides the public policy with publisher information. The hosting provider’s practices also apply when visiting external pages.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub login. GitHub’s processing follows its privacy statement. These dedicated PerUse pages have no advertising, analytics, external fonts, contact form, or developer-added cookies or browser storage. Email and external pages open when you choose their links.

Changes

This policy will be reviewed on this page if the app’s data processing changes.
