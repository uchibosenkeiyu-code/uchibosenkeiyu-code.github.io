Support & FAQ

Are the items shown on first launch my records?

The five initial items are samples for exploring the app. The Exploring samples banner identifies this mode. Recording, items, and insights use either samples or your own records. Adding your own item switches to My records. Sample recurring costs, target counts, insight amounts, and recording queues stay separate. Editing a sample does not turn it into a personal item; add a new item for your actual records.

Can I return to the samples?

Use Data shown to switch. The choice persists after reopening. Switching does not delete your own records or combine them with samples. Show samples in Manage data also restores missing sample identities when requested. It does not overwrite existing items, samples, or history.

Why are amounts still in yen when I use English?

The app manages amounts in Japanese yen. Changing the interface language does not convert values into USD or another currency.

What do Used and Not used do?

Used adds one usage record. Not used skips this week's review without adding a use. Automatic daily tracking uses calendar days from the start date. Insights compare costs per use and costs per day separately.

Does archiving cancel a subscription?

No. It pauses management and calculations within the app. Cancel contracts with each service directly. Annual cost of archived subscriptions is an annualized amount based on your entered prices, not confirmation of cancellation or actual savings.

The photo suggestions are wrong.

Review the on-device suggestions and edit the name and amount before saving. When several amounts are found, you may need to choose one. Photos and camera use are optional; manual entry is available. Change camera permission in iOS Settings. The app does not modify original library photos or automatically save camera images to the library.

How can I delete data? Will resetting bring samples back?

Delete in item details removes the item and related history. Reset all data in Manage data removes both personal records and samples, together with their history. Confirm carefully because these actions cannot be undone. Samples are not automatically reinserted after reset or on the next launch. Choose Show samples in Manage data explicitly if you want to recreate them. Original images and existing OS backups are managed separately.

Can I transfer or restore records?

The app does not provide its own synchronization, export, or backup restoration. OS backup migration depends on your settings and circumstances. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or restore your local records.

Are there paid features or ads?

The initial release is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Release builds have no Pro entry, planned-price offer, or route to a coming-soon purchase sheet. Existing features have no Pro restrictions. An explicitly labeled future UI is retained only in development Debug builds, without a payment implementation.

Where can I read about privacy?

Open Privacy and data from Manage data in Items for offline technical information about storage, photos, backups, deletion and transfers. The Privacy page on this site contains the policy with publisher information.

How can I change the language?

The app follows its preferred language setting in iOS and supports Japanese and English. If an app language selector is unavailable, also check the device's preferred languages.

How should I report a problem?

Use the contact below to report the app version, iOS version, device model, steps and expected result. Prefer fictional names and amounts. Redact personal information from photos and receipts. Do not send passwords, credentials or device passcodes.

How can I report an issue in a TestFlight beta?

Choose Send Beta Feedback from the app's TestFlight page and include the version/build, steps, expected result, and actual result. This optional submission uses Apple's beta-testing service and shares your comments and attachments with Apple and the developer. It is separate from the app's local item storage and does not mean that the app automatically uploads your records. Use fictional data in screenshots, and do not attach personal photos, real receipts, or credentials. You may also email the support contact on this page.
