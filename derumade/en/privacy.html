Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026

This policy explains how uchibosenkeiyu handles information in the iPhone app Get Ready to Go and on this dedicated support website.

1. Preparation settings and records stay on your device

The app stores plan and step names, durations, departure times and weekdays, completion and exclusion actions, changes, estimate candidates, and OS scheduling identifiers locally. The app and widget extension share this state through an App Group dedicated to this app.

There is no developer account system, custom server, cloud sync, advertising, external analytics SDK, or external AI. Preparation settings and action records are not automatically sent to the developer. The app does not read location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or calendar data.

2. Alarms and Live Activities

Get Ready to Go uses Apple's AlarmKit, ActivityKit, and WidgetKit to register guidance and departure alarms with the OS and show step names and departure dates on the Lock Screen and other system surfaces. Change permissions and presentation in iPhone Settings. If you do not want routine details shown while locked, avoid personal information in step names and review display settings or enabled plans.

Revoking permission does not delete locally saved plans and records. To cancel a whole recurring schedule, delete that plan in the app.

3. Adjusting time estimates

Estimate candidates are calculated on your device from action records. They are not precise measurements of actual work. Turn automatic adjustment off or set a fixed duration if preferred.

The app uses system uptime locally to compare elapsed time and detect changes to the device clock. Raw uptime and derived information are not sent to the developer.

4. Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles Pro purchases, restoration, and entitlement verification. The app evaluates verified product, transaction, and revocation information locally. The developer does not receive payment card details.

Apple's transaction processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy and service terms. The app does not send transactions to a custom server to check access.

5. Retention and deletion

Preparation history retains up to 30 recent runs and up to 20 recent time candidates per step. OS scheduling and recovery records may remain separately from these history limits. An on-device copy of the previous saved state is also kept for recovery from corruption.

Time estimates > Reset records and learning resets the selected plan's records and learning. Saved plans and your fixed estimates are kept. State needed for active guidance and a recovery copy may remain. Deleting a schedule differs from resetting history.

To remove all local app data, cancel schedules and use Delete App in iPhone Settings. Offload App can retain data. Copies included in OS backups are managed by your iCloud or other backup settings and Apple's system behavior. This is not custom cloud sync, and the developer cannot delete device or backup data remotely.

6. Support requests

If you choose to email support, your email address, message, and attachments are used to reply and investigate the issue. They are retained as needed to handle the request; information no longer needed is deleted. You may request deletion through the contact below. Email providers may process messages for delivery and storage.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment information, or unnecessary personal routine records. You can use the app without sending an email.

7. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub privacy statement.

The developer has added no advertising, analytics, external fonts, or contact form to these dedicated pages. Email and external links open when you choose them.

8. Updates and contact

This page will be updated when the app or its information practices change. Material changes will be announced in the app or on the support page.
