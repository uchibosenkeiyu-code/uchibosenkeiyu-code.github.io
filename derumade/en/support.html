Support

Updated October 24, 2026 · iPhone / iOS 26 or later

Get Ready to Go guides your preparation with what is now, next, and after that.

Set up your preparation

  1. Choose a template, departure time, and weekdays. Edit step estimates and buffer if needed.
  2. Check the next departure and guidance dates, then tap Set up this preparation. Displaying unconfirmed defaults does not schedule an alarm.
  3. Allow alarms when prompted. If scheduling fails, your input is kept and you can retry.

Departure alarms can sound in Silent and Focus modes. Guidance is not promised to be completely silent, and Lock Screen actions may require authentication.

Complete steps in your own order

Use Done for the step you finished. Completing an upcoming step leaves the current step unfinished. Open the app for all steps, undo, choosing the current step, and changes or ordering limited to this run.

Time passing never completes a step or automatically advances to the next step.

When the remaining time is unknown

If the estimate expires and the step is still unfinished, remaining work is unknown. Choose another 1, 3, or 5 minutes, or enter a duration, measured from now. Completion times and time to spare are estimates. Get Ready to Go never moves your departure automatically.

If guidance does not appear or an action is unavailable

  • Check that the plan is enabled, its weekday and concrete dates are correct, and the schedule says Applied to the OS schedule. Saving differs from successful OS scheduling.
  • Check that alarms and Live Activities are allowed for Get Ready to Go in iPhone Settings.
  • Wake the Lock Screen and authenticate if requested. A normally locked screen differs from requiring authentication to launch the app itself.
  • Apple does not support AlarmKit for hidden apps or apps configured to require authentication on launch.
  • After restarting and before the first unlock, the OS may use a system presentation. Presentation and actions depend on iOS and device state.

Avoid repeatedly recreating a schedule solely to check visibility, as unwanted reservations may remain. Check the scheduling result and contact support if the issue continues.

Change today or end this run

After guidance starts, today's changes and order sheet lets you adjust the current run. Changing only today's departure is separate from the normal weekday rule. If synchronization fails, read the message and use Retry OS update when offered.

End this run ends the current guidance without marking unfinished steps complete. To cancel the whole recurring rule, delete that plan in Edit plans.

Time estimates and learning

In Time estimates, turn automatic adjustment off or set a fixed duration. After five eligible candidates from different runs, their median can adjust future estimates. Intervals between actions are not precise measurements of work; waiting and parallel activity cannot always be distinguished.

Adjustments do not alter the active run or its departure. Saved estimates and pending OS schedule updates are shown separately.

Free and Get Ready to Go Pro

One saved plan, all templates, step editing, weekday repeats, Live Activities, and estimate adjustments are free. Pro adds saving and using multiple plans with a one-time purchase and no monthly fee. Only one schedule can be active on a given weekday.

See the current StoreKit price in the app. Open the Pro screen and Restore purchase from Edit plans. Use the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If your entitlement changes, saved plans are not automatically deleted; you can choose one free plan.

Delete records or app data

Time estimates > Reset records and learning resets the selected plan's records and learning. Saved plans and fixed estimates are kept. State needed for active guidance and an on-device recovery copy may remain.

Deleting a plan differs from resetting records. See the privacy policy for removing all local app data and handling OS backups. There is no custom cloud sync or export feature.
